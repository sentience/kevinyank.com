In [Subspace Radio \#95](https://www.subspace.fm/episodes/episode-95-occupations-snw-4x04-a-case-of-chiaroscuro), Robert Lloyd and I take the zeppelin to Chinatown (or was it the shuttle to Mat'Aar XII?) and do our best to blend in while we discuss “A Case of Chiaroscuro” (SNW). After banishing the local occupying force with the power of song, we consider other occupations throughout Star Trek history, including “Errand of Mercy” (TOS) and “Things Past” (DS9).
