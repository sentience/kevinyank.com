In [Subspace Radio \#97](https://www.subspace.fm/episodes/episode-97-cmo-careers-snw-4x06-off-hour), Robert Lloyd and I argue over which of us is most likely to quit the podcast first as the ship’s chronometer counts down to the start of “Off-Hour” (SNW). Afterward, we trace the career paths of each of the Enterprise’s Chief Medical Officers, with stops for “Encounter at Farpoint” (TNG), “Disaster” (TNG), “Unnatural Selection” (TNG), “Virtuoso” (VOY), and “Dear Doctor” (ENT).
