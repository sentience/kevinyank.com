In [Subspace Radio #102](https://www.subspace.fm/episodes/episode-102-remembering-walter-koenig-patrick-stewart), Robert Lloyd and I reunite during the hiatus to remember Walter Koenig and Patrick Stewart. We share our favourite performances, including “Spectre of the Gun” (TOS), “Star Trek II: The Wrath of Khan”, “Star Trek IV: The Voyage Home”, “The Measure of a Man” (TNG), “Star Trek: First Contact”, “Family” (TNG), “The Inner Light” (TNG), and “Lessons” (TNG).
