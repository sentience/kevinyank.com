In [Subspace Radio #101](https://www.subspace.fm/episodes/episode-101-fragile-connections-snw-4x10-tomorrows-enterprise), Robert Lloyd and I are separated by a wormhole and a year of time displacement, but still manage to talk Star Trek. After my visit to the Original Series set tour in Ticonderoga, we discuss “Tomorrow’s Enterprise” (SNW) and two more episodes with fragile communications links: “Pen Pals” (TNG) and “Eye of the Needle” (VOY).
