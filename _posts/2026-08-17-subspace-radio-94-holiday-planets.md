In [Subspace Radio \#94](https://www.subspace.fm/episodes/episode-94-holiday-planets-snw-4x03-human-best-friend), Robert Lloyd and I take the week off. Actually, it was meant to be just a long weekend, but in the haze that was “Human Best Friend”, time sort of got away from us. While chilling by the pool, we reflect on other occasions where crews took a well-deserved break, including “Shore Leave” (TOS) and “Let He Who Is Without Sin…” (DS9).
